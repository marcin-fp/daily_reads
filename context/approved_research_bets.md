### **C-scale: capability challenge rating**

| Rating | Name | Definition | Typical profile |
| :---: | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **C1** | Incremental Capability | Improve an existing capability or workflow using known approaches. | Low uncertainty; localized impact; clear path to ship. |
| **C2** | Applied Extension | Adapt a known pattern to a new workflow, dataset, domain, or product surface. | Moderate uncertainty; adjacent impact; product path mostly clear. |
| **C3** | New Capability Bet | Create a capability that is new to us and not a straightforward implementation of known work. | High uncertainty; meaningful impact; requires evidence and validation. |
| **C4** | Platform / Paradigm Bet | Build reusable foundations or new ways of working that can reshape multiple products or research areas. | Very high uncertainty; high leverage; architecture or platform implications. |
| **C5** | Frontier / Field-Shaping Bet | Pursue breakthroughs that could redefine scientific research workflows or create a new category. | Extreme uncertainty; transformational upside; field or company defining. |

**Calibration rule.** Rate company-specific uncertainty and strategic upside against the external frontier. C4/C5 may reflect potential; S-stage records whether that potential is only an opportunity, a tested hypothesis, or a validated capability.

IMPACT PATHSPrimary A \- a reusable baseline and evaluation harness. Secondary L \- decisive architecture learning. Secondary R \- credible public comparisons and a possible open-source reference. STAGE AND TARGETCurrent: S1 Hypothesis. Target: S3 Validated capability within 2 quarters. ## **B03  Math-function embeddings for retrieval, comparison, and context engineering**

| Learn function-level representations that retrieve and compare mathematically related objects across notation, equivalence transformations, derivations, and surrounding scientific context. | C4-A/P/S, S1, 2-4Q, Medium-High confidence |
| :---- | :---: |

| PORTFOLIO BUCKETPlatform / Strategic Bet C-RATING RATIONALEWhile academic attempts at formula retrieval exist, a large-scale system indexing massive datasets remains unprecedented. The C4 potential lies in a shared representation layer that reshapes retrieval, context construction, verification, derivation search, and cross-domain analogy. Furthermore, formulas serve as an entry point into multi-modal data search and processing methods applicable across various scientific domains. IMPACT PATHSPrimary A \- Novel embedding model(s), a benchmark for evaluating retrieval relevance, and an indexed mathematical corpus. Secondary P \- Enhanced search capabilities and context packs; potentially the first equation search engine indexing mainstream STEM resources. Secondary S \- A foundation for multi-modal reasoning and processing, enabling AI systems distinct from mainstream LLMs and VLMs. STAGE AND TARGETCurrent: S1 Prototype. Several promising methods have been identified, but additional compute is required for extensive testing. Target: S3 Validated capability (2-4 quarters) with quantifiable metrics for search efficacy. S2 Hypothesis: Explore novel applications for mathematical embeddings. MISSION LINKEnables the system to identify critical mathematics regardless of notation, vocabulary, or derivation style. Connecting scientific literature beyond text synergizes with the goal of a unified physics framework. CAPABILITY BETRepresent mathematical functions and symbolic objects such that algebraic rewriting, parameterization changes, and notation shifts do not obscure functional relationships, while maintaining the clarity of domains, assumptions, and units. CURRENT FRONTIERCurrent math information retrieval systems rely primarily on sparse, feature-based methods. Embedding-based approaches are rare and have not yet demonstrated efficacy at scale. | HYPOTHESISA multi-modal "text+math" embedding can retrieve cross-domain results otherwise ignored by conventional retrievers. These results will enable novel reasoning chains and ideation previously unavailable to AI systems. FIRST DECISIVE EXPERIMENTBenchmark several retrieval methods using ARQMATH (Math Stack Exchange). Measure cross-modal equation discovery by comparing our embedding methods against standard BM25 baselines. EVIDENCE PLANEvaluate nDCG@10 and Recall@k on standard benchmarks (NTCIR, ARQMATH). For tasks without ground-truths (e.g., arXiv cross-modal matching), use LLM-as-judge and UMBRELA-style evaluations. Test LLM ideation gains via arena comparisons with and without the novel retrieval scheme. TRANSLATION PATHPrimary users include academic search platforms and agentic literature search systems. Researchers can leverage these AIs to discover new mathematical processing methods or extend work into adjacent domains. ASSET / PUBLICATION / IP PATHA comprehensive dataset of STEM literature featuring parsed papers and equations, alongside large-scale indexes of intra-document components. KILL OR PIVOT CRITERIADo not scale if hybrid lexical-plus-symbolic baselines match the performance of classic retrieval (e.g., BM25) or if retrieval gains fail to improve reasoning. Pivot to scaling if significant ideation improvements are observed or if similar methods prove effective for other modalities like graphs or proofs. COLLABORATORS NEEDEDExperts in mathematical representation, mathematicians, retrieval specialists, formal methods researchers, and physicists capable of defining equivalence classes. |

## **B04  Universal physics-concept embeddings**

| Develop a universal representation of physical concepts across language, equations, units, diagrams, simulations, experiments, scales, and regimes rather than a text-only physics embedding. | C5-A/S/R, S0, 6-12Q, Low confidence |
| :---- | :---: |

| PORTFOLIO BUCKETFrontier / Reputation Bet C-RATING RATIONALEA representation that exposes shared physical structure across subfields and modalities could become a new foundation layer for scientific AI and materially shape the field. IMPACT PATHSPrimary A \- a universal concept space and training corpus. Secondary S \- common interface for models and tools. Secondary R \- field-shaping publication and IP potential. STAGE AND TARGETCurrent: S0 Opportunity. Target: S2 Prototype over 6-12 quarters. MISSION LINKProvides the conceptual substrate an AI physicist would need to recognize the same physical idea across different theories, modalities, scales, and vocabularies. CAPABILITY BETLearn embeddings that align concepts such as oscillation, diffusion, symmetry breaking, conservation, phase, field, and transport across text, equations, simulations, diagrams, and observations while preserving units, assumptions, causal direction, and validity regimes. CURRENT FRONTIERPhysBERT is a physics-specific text embedding model, while the Universal New Physics Latent Space maps a narrow family of collider theories into one phenomenological space. Symmetry-aware representation learning is active, and new multimodal scientific models align several data types, but no demonstrated representation is universal across physics concepts, modalities, scales, and regimes. \[W15-W17, W24\] | HYPOTHESISCarefully chosen cross-domain physical structures can be aligned in a shared latent space without erasing the distinctions that determine when an analogy is valid. FIRST DECISIVE EXPERIMENTStart with three cross-domain concept families: oscillators across mechanics, circuits, and quantum systems; diffusion across heat, particles, and stochastic processes; and waves across acoustics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics. Train multi-view contrastive and equivariant representations and evaluate analogy, transfer, and regime sensitivity. EVIDENCE PLANCross-modal retrieval, zero-shot transfer, concept analogy accuracy, symmetry and invariant probes, unit and regime discrimination, expert-authored concept maps, and resistance to false analogies. TRANSLATION PATHServe as a common memory and interface layer for literature, world models, surrogate models, verifiers, conjecturing, and the Collaborator. ASSET / PUBLICATION / IP PATHPhysics ontology, multimodal aligned corpus, evaluation suite, embedding model, latent-space visualization tools, publications, and potentially foundational IP. KILL OR PIVOT CRITERIAPivot to explicit knowledge graphs plus domain-specific embeddings if the shared space collapses important distinctions, fails to transfer, or cannot outperform metadata-rich retrieval. COLLABORATORS NEEDEDTheoretical and experimental physicists across several fields, representation-learning researchers, ontology and data specialists, scientific visualization, and evaluation experts. |

## **B05  World models for physics**

| Build intervention-capable latent models of physical systems that can simulate, predict, plan, and test counterfactuals across multiple forms of physics with calibrated uncertainty. | C5-A/S/P, S1, 6-12Q, Medium-Low confidence |
| :---- | :---: |

| PORTFOLIO BUCKETFrontier / Reputation Bet C-RATING RATIONALEA general scientific world model would move beyond task-specific surrogates toward a new category of reasoning and simulation infrastructure. IMPACT PATHSPrimary A \- reusable world-model architecture and physical simulation corpus. Secondary S \- new scientific-computing platform. Secondary P \- interactive simulation and counterfactual reasoning. STAGE AND TARGETCurrent: S1 Hypothesis. Target: S2 Prototype over 6-12 quarters. MISSION LINKGives the scientific system an internal medium for imagining and testing physical consequences rather than relying only on longer chains of text. CAPABILITY BETLearn a structured latent state and transition model conditioned on system description, geometry, boundary and initial conditions, interventions, scale, and uncertainty. ASSET / PUBLICATION / IP PATHMulti-physics trajectory corpus, system-description schema, model, simulation benchmark, uncertainty layer, and high-value publication and patent opportunities. KILL OR PIVOT CRITERIAPivot to a federation of specialist models if the generalist materially underperforms specialists, fails physical invariants, or cannot generalize beyond superficial dataset correlations. ## **B06  JEPA for Physics**

| Create a physics-native joint-embedding predictive architecture that learns hidden physical structure, state transitions, and multiscale organization in latent space rather than reconstructing pixels or tokens. | C5-A/R/S, S1, 4-8Q, Medium confidence |
| :---- | :---: |

| PORTFOLIO BUCKETFrontier / Reputation Bet C-RATING RATIONALEApplying an existing JEPA recipe to one dataset is C3 or C4; inventing a physics-native JEPA objective that transfers across systems, scales, and modalities could be field-shaping. IMPACT PATHSPrimary A \- new representation objective, model family, and datasets. Secondary R \- strong publication and scientific leadership. Secondary S \- foundation for world models and universal physics representations. STAGE AND TARGETCurrent: S1 Hypothesis. Target: S2 Prototype over 4-8 quarters. MISSION LINKTests whether predictive latent learning can discover useful physical coordinates and dynamics without forcing the model to reconstruct every observational detail. CAPABILITY BETPredict masked or future physical representations across space, time, scale, and intervention, with objectives and masking geometry informed by symmetries, conserved quantities, causal structure, and multiscale fields. CURRENT FRONTIERV-JEPA 2 and 2.1 demonstrate large-scale latent video prediction and physical planning. Domain-specific scientific work is now appearing: HEP-JEPA for collider jets, Phys-JEPA for physics-informed latent time-series transitions, and ScaleAware-JEPA for multiscale physical fields. The evidence is promising but narrow and largely preliminary; no universal physics JEPA has been demonstrated. \[W4-W8\] | HYPOTHESISA latent prediction objective aligned to physical structure will learn more transferable, stable, and scientifically meaningful representations than masked reconstruction or next-step output prediction at matched compute. FIRST DECISIVE EXPERIMENTTrain matched models on particle and continuous-field datasets using reconstruction, autoregressive, and JEPA objectives. Use context-target masks across space, time, and scale; add latent consistency for invariants and compare transfer to held-out dynamics and interventions. EVIDENCE PLANSample efficiency, long-horizon stability, zero-shot transfer, linear probes for conserved quantities and physical state, planning performance, OOD detection, and interpretability of latent neighborhoods. TRANSLATION PATHUse successful JEPA encoders and predictors as the representation engine for physics world models, concept embeddings, surrogate interfaces, and counterfactual planning. ASSET / PUBLICATION / IP PATHPretraining objective, model checkpoints, multiscale datasets, probes and benchmarks, open-source components, publications, and possible foundational patents. KILL OR PIVOT CRITERIADo not scale if JEPA does not beat strong autoencoder, masked-model, or neural-operator baselines on transfer and stability, or if the latent state remains physically unstructured. COLLABORATORS NEEDEDSelf-supervised learning and JEPA researchers, scientific ML, particle and field physicists, interpretability, simulation, and evaluation engineering. |
